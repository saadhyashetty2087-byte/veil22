What the shield can and cannot do

What we are testing

In one preliminary Stable Diffusion v1.5 test, processed edits showed visible distortion; one original edit was also distorted. More testing is needed.

Known limits

Meta successfully edited a processed photo in one reported test. Veil does not guarantee protection against editors, face swaps or video deepfakes.

The seal is a signal

A detected seal is an unauthenticated signal; it can be forged or lost. It cannot prove identity, consent, or prevent editing, and is not legally binding by itself.

Experimental student demo. Use only photos you have permission to process. Compression, resizing and cropping may weaken the shield and seal.
